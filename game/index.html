LANTERN LANES
Duel of the Three Bridges

Three bridges. Three lanterns. One Keeper to outwit.
Play spirits onto the bridges — a spirit on its own bridge gains +2 power (or +3 while you trail on life).
Tricks bend a lane: Flare +3, Douse −3, Mend +5 life.
Each round the strongest side of a bridge deals the difference (max 5) as damage. First lantern out loses.

Both sides: 20 life · 5-card hand · energy grows each round · the Keeper announces the bridge it eyes — counter it.

Mouse or keyboard: 1–5 select · Q/W/E place · Space end turn

★ VICTORY ★

Every bridge burns bright. The Keeper bows, and the lanterns are yours.

DEFEAT

Your last lantern gutters out. The Keeper keeps the bridges — this time.
